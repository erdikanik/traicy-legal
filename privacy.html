Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Traicy app (the "Application") for mobile devices, made by Erdi Kanık (the "Service Provider"). It explains what information the Application collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have.

Effective date: 2026-11-03

Who is responsible

The Service Provider, Erdi Kanık, is the controller of your personal data. Contact: erdikanik@gmail.com.

Information the Application collects

  • The name you enter so that the characters can call you by it.
  • Your chat messages and the characters' replies. They are stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) together with your name and a random identifier of the installation, so that the service can work and be improved, and to keep it safe.
  • Notes the characters keep about your chats (for example your interests or plans you mentioned), your app settings, levels, and the photos the characters send you. These are kept on your device.
  • Usage and device information, such as the screens and features you use, the time spent in the Application, your device model, operating system, language, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city). The Application does not collect your precise location.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics.
  • Purchase information: which subscription you have and its status. Payments are handled by Apple; the Service Provider never sees your payment details.
  • Advertising identifiers: the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when iOS asks you (App Tracking Transparency). Without your permission, ads are measured without it.

How your chat is answered (AI processing)

The characters are powered by artificial intelligence. To write each reply, your message, the recent chat, the notes mentioned above and the character's description are sent through the Service Provider's server (Google Cloud Functions) to an AI model provider, DeepSeek. The AI provider uses them to generate the reply. Your chats are not used for advertising and are not shared with advertisers. Please do not share sensitive information (such as health data, passwords or financial details) in the chat.

Advertising

Free use of the Application is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, including short ads you can choose to watch for extra features. AdMob may use device identifiers, your IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalized; you can change your choice at any time in the Application's Settings under "Privacy Choices". On iOS, ads are personalized with the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.

Why your data is used (legal bases)

  • To provide the chat and the Application's features, and your subscription (performance of a contract).
  • To personalize ads and to use the advertising identifier (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To understand how the Application is used, fix crashes, prevent abuse and keep the service secure (legitimate interests).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

The Application uses the following services, which process data on the Service Provider's behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions)
  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • DeepSeek (AI replies)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • Meta (app events and ad measurement)
  • Unity (3D engine)

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States and, for AI replies, in China. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

The Service Provider may also disclose information when required by law, or when necessary to protect their rights, your safety or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud.

Data retention

Chats and other data are kept for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter, unless you ask for them to be deleted sooner. Data kept on your device is removed when you delete the Application.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing before the withdrawal. To exercise these rights, including deleting your chats, email erdikanik@gmail.com with the name you use in the Application. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

  • Ad personalization: Settings > Privacy Choices in the Application (where offered).
  • Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Notifications: iOS Settings > Notifications.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case the Service Provider discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider (erdikanik@gmail.com) so that they will be able to take the necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider uses reasonable physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect the information they process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider at erdikanik@gmail.com.
